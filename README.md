# A2A.org

**Adolescent to Adult Neuro-Oncology Research** — A fictional non-profit research institution advancing transition science, neurocognition, and quality of life for adolescents and young adults with brain and spinal cord tumors.

Inspired by the work of **Jessica D. Schulte, MD, PhD** ([NYU Langone](https://nyulangone.org/doctors/1467870576/jessica-d-schulte)), Director of the Adolescent and Young Adult Neuro-Oncology program at NYU Langone Brain & Spine Tumor Center.

## Content

- Set in a near-future scenario (2028–2029) imagining major breakthroughs in AYA CNS transition readiness and neuroprotective trials.
- References real initiatives: PNOC long-term outcome study, SNO AYA guidelines and special interest track, peer-led support models.

## Tech

- Static HTML/CSS, no build step.
- Deploy to any static host (e.g. Vercel, Netlify).
